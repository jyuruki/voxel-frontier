NORTHSHIRE VALLEY
An independent, solo browser recreation of the World of Warcraft Classic human starter experience. World of Warcraft and Warcraft belong to Blizzard Entertainment.

Artwork
Northshire title painting and Home Screen artwork created for this project using OpenAI image generation.
The playable landscape, structures, figures, materials and animations were authored for this browser edition. Character portraits are original generated paintings.
