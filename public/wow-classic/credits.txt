NORTHSHIRE VALLEY
An independent, solo browser recreation of the World of Warcraft Classic human starter experience. World of Warcraft and Warcraft belong to Blizzard Entertainment.

Artwork
Northshire title painting and Home Screen artwork created for this project using OpenAI image generation.
The landscape, buildings, character portraits, class colors and heraldry were created or adapted for this browser edition. Character and creature models and skeletal animations are adapted from Kay Lousberg’s KayKit Adventurers and Quaternius’s CC0 animated models. Full model credits and source links: art/world/CREDITS.txt.
